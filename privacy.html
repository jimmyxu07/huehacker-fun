Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Hue Hacker is a static client-side tool. We don’t have servers collecting your data, and we don’t run analytics trackers.

What we don’t collect

  • No personal information (name, email, address).
  • We do not use cookies or local storage for our own tracking.
  • We use GoatCounter, a privacy-first analytics tool that does not use cookies or track personal information. It collects anonymous visit counts to help us understand site usage.
  • No behavioral tracking or fingerprinting by us.

Third-party advertising

We display ads through Adsterra, a third-party advertising network, to help support the site.

Adsterra may use cookies, device identifiers, IP addresses, browser information, and page visit data to:

  • Show relevant ads based on your interests (interest-based advertising)
  • Measure ad performance and prevent fraud
  • Deliver ads using IFRAME SYNC and NATIVE ASYNC technologies

Ad formats you may see include banner ads (728×90, 320×50), square ads (300×250), and native banners.

These activities are governed by Adsterra’s own privacy practices. You can review their policy here: Adsterra Privacy Policy

Cookie policy

Our site itself does not set cookies. However, third parties may place cookies when you visit:

Type Purpose Set by
Necessary Required for the site to function (none set by us) —
Analytics Anonymous visit counts without personal data GoatCounter (cookieless)
Advertising Ad delivery, personalization, and measurement Adsterra

Your browser allows you to block or delete cookies at any time.

Your choices

You have control over ad-related tracking:

  • Browser settings: You can disable third-party cookies in your browser settings to limit tracking.
  • Adsterra opt-out: Visit Adsterra’s privacy policy for their opt-out options.
  • Industry tools: You can also use these independent tools to manage interest-based advertising:
    • Your Online Choices (EU)
    • Network Advertising Initiative (US)
    • Digital Advertising Alliance (US)

What may be logged (hosting)

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub may log standard server data such as your IP address, browser type, and requested URLs for operational and security purposes. We do not have direct access to these logs. See GitHub’s Privacy Statement for details.

Changes

We may update this policy if the site’s architecture or ad partnerships change. Updates will be posted here with a new effective date.
